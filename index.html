Introduce myself like a product

01- THE PROBLEM

How do I introduce myself to the right people in a way that shows both what I can do and who I am – without turning this into another boring portfolio bio?

02- THE GOAL

Help a potential employer understand who I am, how I think, and what I bring to a team as quickly as possible – and hopefully make them curious enough to know me better.

03- PERSONA

HAMID REZA MOHAMMADI

Age: 29

Background: Cinema - MA

Role: Product Designer

Based in: Tehran

I have always been interested in creating ideas and discovering problems.
During my undergraduate studies in Theatre, I moved toward dramaturgy because I wanted to understand how ideas are built, structured, and communicated — almost like being the architect of an idea.
After COVID, I decided to start an online business of my own. I needed a website, so I trusted someone to design it for me. A few months later, I discovered that they had, well... slightly exaggerated their web design skills.
So I started learning design myself.
What began as a necessity gradually became a serious interest in web design, UI/UX, and eventually product design.
My background in theatre and dramaturgy taught me to look beyond the obvious problem, understand different perspectives, and search for the story underneath the surface. Today, whenever I encounter a new problem, I get excited by one question:
"How many different ways can I solve this?"

GOALS

Solve meaningful problems through design.

Turn ideas and insights into useful digital products.

Keep learning and expanding my design skills.

Work with experienced people and learn from them.

Build products that are both functional and thoughtfully designed.

Meet more people from the professional design community.

NEEDS

Opportunities to work on real product problems.

Feedback from experienced designers and teams.

A space where I can experiment, learn, and contribute.

People who value curiosity, ideas, and problem-solving.

A chance to show not only what I can design, but how I think.

MOTIVATIONS

Curiosity I enjoy discovering how things work and why problems exist.
Creating Ideas Coming up with different possibilities is one of the parts of design I enjoy most.
Problem Solving A new problem feels less like an obstacle and more like an invitation to think.
People & Culture I enjoy meeting new people, making new friends, and learning from different perspectives.
Art & Cinema I still spend a lot of my time around films, theatre, and other forms of art. Apparently, I never really left.

PAIN POINTS

Sometimes I have been too modest about my own work.

My background is not the most conventional path into product design.

I have often had to explain how theatre, dramaturgy, and design connect.

Getting my work in front of the right people has not always been easy.

04- DESIGN REQUIREMENTS

To reach these goals, I needed a portfolio that could do four things:

01 — Show the work
Make my projects easy to discover and understand.

02 — Tell the story
Present my background, experience, and the way I think — not just the final designs.

03 — Introduce the person behind the work
Create an About section that gives people the information they need while still feeling personal and engaging.

04 — Make contact easy
Give visitors a clear and simple way to get in touch with me.

The idea
A portfolio should not only answer "What can Hamid design?"
It should also answer: "Who is Hamid, how does he think, and would I like to work with him?"
