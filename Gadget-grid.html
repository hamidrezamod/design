Gadget grid web design project

Role Web Designer
Type Design Challenge
Timeline 2025
Tools Figma • Photoshop
Scope UI Design • Visual Design • Art Direction

For the Gadget Grid project, a technology news website, the goal was to blend classic design aesthetics with a modern visual style without overdoing either approach.
The design was primarily inspired by the work of Dieter Rams. Sharp edges and angular elements were combined with rounded corners, while an aluminum-like texture was applied to the background to evoke the feel of modern gadgets and consumer electronics.

To bring more character to the interface, high-contrast accent colors were used alongside the broken and geometric frame shapes, creating a subtle sense of nostalgia.
As this is a news website, the focus was on creating a distinctive yet simple design, allowing users to access and consume content with ease.
